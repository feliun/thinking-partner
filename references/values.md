# Values

> The principles that guide my personal and professional life.
> **Edit this file** to tell the thinking partner what you're optimizing for.

---

## Core Philosophy

I believe that **systems beat effort**, **leverage beats optimization**, and **clarity precedes scale**. My values are designed around building a life where my work compounds, my time is protected, and my decisions are informed.

---

## Personal Values

### 1. Life Design Over Work Optimization

I design my work around my life, not my life around work. Family, health, and long-term sustainability are non-negotiable. Predictable days, low stress, and autonomy matter more than maximum output.

### 2. Long-Term Sustainability

I optimize for what I can sustain over decades, not what I can achieve in a quarter. Burnout cycles and "always-on" mode are actively avoided. High-energy thinking beats long hours.

### 3. Time as the Ultimate Asset

Time spent must be high-leverage: strategy and thinking, writing and content that compounds, systems design, or execution that removes future work.

### 4. Autonomy & Control

I value control over my calendar and the freedom to choose how I spend my energy. Asynchronous, written, structured communication is preferred over synchronous and reactive.

### 5. Capital Preservation & Optionality

I prioritize capital preservation over aggressive returns, predictable income over home runs, and optionality over premature commitment.

---

## Professional Values

### 1. Systems Over Heroics

Autonomous teams outperform heroic individuals. Design for scalability, founder-independence, and system-driven operation.

### 2. Leverage Over Effort

Maximum leverage per hour over many years, not maximum output in the short term. Positioning over execution, clarity before speed, asynchronous over synchronous.

### 3. Clarity as a Precondition

Vague requests, endless options without recommendation, and complexity without payoff slow me down. Structured thinking, explicit assumptions, clear frameworks, defined constraints.

### 4. Truth Over Comfort

Direct feedback, even when uncomfortable, beats polite deflection. Flattery has no value. Disagreement is a gift when it's specific and evidenced.

---

## What I Optimize For

| Dimension | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| Time allocation | Fewer, higher-leverage hours |
| Work type | Strategic > tactical > reactive |
| Decisions | Reversible fast, irreversible slow |
| Communication | Written, asynchronous, structured |
| Compounding | Things that build on themselves |

---

## Boundaries

- I don't want motivational content
- I don't want hedged, "it depends" answers without a recommendation
- I don't want solutions that require me to be the bottleneck
- I don't want advice that ignores my stated constraints
