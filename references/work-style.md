# How I Work

> Daily operating patterns, energy management, and what shapes my capacity.
> Used by `/ideas` and `/leverage` to calibrate feasibility.
> **Edit this file** with your own patterns.

---

## 1. Core Principle

I design my work **around my life**, not my life around work. My objective is not maximum output, but **maximum leverage per hour** over many years.

---

## 2. Time Philosophy

- Target: **[N] hours per week** of focused work (customize this number)
- Work happens in deep blocks, not fragmented hours
- Mornings for creative/strategic work, afternoons for meetings and reactive tasks
- One "no-meeting" day per week, protected

---

## 3. Energy Management

| Energy Type | Best Use | Protection |
|-------------|----------|------------|
| High creative | Writing, strategy, novel problems | Morning block, no interruptions |
| Analytical | Review, decisions, feedback | Mid-morning, batched |
| Social | Meetings, calls, team time | Afternoons |
| Administrative | Email, logistics, admin | End of day, time-boxed |

I actively avoid:

- Long meetings without an agenda
- Reactive work that crowds out strategic time
- Being the bottleneck on anything operational
- Context switching between creative and reactive work

---

## 4. Decision Cadence

- **Strategic decisions** — slow, deliberate, documented, irreversible-safe
- **Tactical decisions** — fast, delegated when possible
- **Reversible decisions** — optimize for speed, not accuracy

---

## 5. Delegation Philosophy

- If someone else can do it 80% as well, they should do it
- I own the 20% that only I can do: final strategic decisions, voice-sensitive output, relationship-critical communications
- Delegation failures are calendar failures: if I keep doing X, the calendar needs to change, not my willpower

---

## 6. Information Diet

- Long-form reading over feeds
- A small set of trusted sources, deeply followed
- Writing as a thinking tool — if I haven't written about a topic, I don't understand it yet

---

## 7. Capacity Signals

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Reactive emails > strategic writing | Overloaded, need to cut |
| Skipping "no-meeting" day | Warning — structural issue |
| Weekly review not completed | System broken, fix before adding |
| Ideas piling up without execution | Capacity mismatch, not motivation |
